# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Academics, researchers, and graduate students from Indonesia and 30+ countries seeking information about the Annual International Conference (AIC) at Universitas Syiah Kuala. Primary users are prospective attendees deciding whether to submit papers and register; secondary users include committee members, reviewers, and past attendees looking up schedules and proceedings.

## Product Purpose

AIC.usk.ac.id serves as the central information hub for the Annual International Conference: communicating conference scope (three tracks across Sciences, Engineering, Environmental & Life Sciences, and Social Sciences), showcasing keynote and invited speakers, publishing call-for-paper details and important dates, enabling registration and paper submission, listing committees, and archiving past conference editions. Success means a prospective attendee can quickly understand the conference's value, identify their track, and complete registration.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative yet warm — the credibility of a long-established international conference (since 2011) combined with the welcoming character of a host university in Aceh. Distinctive green-and-gold identity rooted in USK's visual heritage. Professional without being cold; prestigious without being exclusionary.

## Anti-references

- Overly corporate SaaS landing pages (HubSpot clones, generic stock-photo hero sections)
- Government/institutional website bloat (excessive menus, small fonts, dense tables)
- Dark edgy "tech conference" tropes (this is an academic conference, not a product launch)
- Cookie-cutter event pages with countdown-as-only-hero

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity first.** Academics scan for deadlines, tracks, and logistics. Layout must surface the critical path (what → when → how to join) without friction.
2. **Identity over template.** The green-gold palette and Poppins typography are distinctive. Protect them from generic conference-website patterns (stock hero images, identical card grids).
3. **Multi-audience calibration.** International researchers need instant credibility signals (16 editions, 30+ countries). Local attendees need clear registration and venue info. Both need readable typography and fast-loading pages.
4. **Content hierarchy over decoration.** Sections earn their place by serving a decision in the attendee journey. Ornament (eyebrows, numbered markers, decorative cards) reduces to what the content needs.
5. **Responsive by default.** Half the audience browses on mobile between meetings. Every layout must work at 375px without horizontal scroll or hidden CTAs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA target. Body text at 4.5:1 minimum contrast. All interactive elements keyboard-accessible. Reduced-motion support already in place via `prefers-reduced-motion`. International audience means clear English with consistent terminology.
